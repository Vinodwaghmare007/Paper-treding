Privacy Policy — Crypto Paper Trader

Last updated: 2026-08-08

Crypto Paper Trader is an educational simulator. No real money, no real exchange account, and no real financial transactions are ever involved — everything the app calls a "balance," "trade," or "P&L" is simulated.

What we collect

Only what's needed to run the simulator and understand which features people actually use:

  • Account info (Firebase Authentication): your email address if you sign up with email/password, or your name, email, and profile photo if you sign in with Google. We never see or store your password itself — Firebase Authentication handles that.
  • Simulated trading data (Cloud Firestore): your simulated balance, simulated buy/sell order history, simulated portfolio holdings, daily-puzzle streak, and (if you use the community chat) the chosen username and messages you post in chat rooms. None of this is real financial data — it never touches a real exchange or bank account.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): which features get used — sign-ups, first simulated trade, daily puzzles played, backtests run, AI forecasts viewed — plus the standard device/ session data Firebase Analytics collects automatically (app version, device type, coarse location such as country, session length). This is aggregate product-usage data, not sold or used for advertising.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): if the app crashes, we receive a stack trace and basic device info to help fix the bug. Crash reporting is off in development builds.

What we don't collect

  • No real payment, bank, or exchange account credentials — the app never asks for any, because it never needs them.
  • No precise/GPS location.
  • No contacts, photos, or files on your device.

Third parties this data passes through

  • Firebase (Google): hosts authentication, the database, analytics, crash reporting, and the backend logic (Cloud Functions) described above. Google's own privacy policy governs how Firebase itself handles data on our behalf: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Binance's public market-data API — used to fetch live/historical crypto prices for the chart, backtester, and puzzle. No personal or account data is ever sent to Binance; these are anonymous, public price-data requests only, and the app never connects to or trades on a real Binance account.
  • Crypto news publisher RSS feeds (CoinDesk, CoinTelegraph, Decrypt, The Block) — our backend polls each publisher's own public RSS feed on a schedule to build the News tab. No personal or account data is ever sent to these publishers; the app itself never contacts them directly, only reads the resulting headlines from our own database. Only a headline, a short truncated summary, source attribution, and a link back to the original article are ever stored or shown — never the full article text.

We do not sell your data to anyone, for advertising or any other purpose.

Deleting your account and data

Open the account menu (the person icon in the top bar of any screen) → Delete account. This is immediate, permanent, and cannot be undone. It removes:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your simulated trade/order history and portfolio
  • Chat messages you've posted (other users' messages in the same room are unaffected)

If you can't access the app to do this yourself, email us (see Contact, below) and we'll delete it for you.

Children

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for users under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
