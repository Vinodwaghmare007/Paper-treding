Crypto Paper Trader for Trading Academies

Give every student a real feel for crypto markets — without a single rupee of real risk.

100% simulated. No real money, no real exchange connection, ever — for you or for any student.

Why academies use it

  • A safe first market. Students place real-time buy/sell orders against live crypto prices, track a live P&L, and feel the emotional pull of a losing streak — all with a simulated balance that resets risk to zero.
  • Built-in discipline tools. A revenge-trading lock, backtesting, a market replay mode, and a discipline score turn "paper trading" into a structured curriculum, not just a free-play sandbox.
  • A cohort view for instructors — without watching over anyone's shoulder. Your admin dashboard shows who's enrolled and an anonymous, class-wide average discipline score. It never shows an individual student's trade history, balance, or orders — that stays private to the student, the same as it would for any other user of the app.

How licensing works

  1. We set your academy up with a seat-based license (trial or paid).
  2. You generate a short invite code from your admin dashboard — one per batch of students, single-use.
  3. Each student enters the code once, during sign-up, to join your cohort and consume a seat.
  4. You see enrollment and an anonymous class average. Nothing more.
